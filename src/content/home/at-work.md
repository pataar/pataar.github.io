I started coding at 14, writing little Windows tweak tools in Visual Basic, and never really stopped. I went on to study computer science and have been building software for a living since 2013.

These days I'm on the [Patient Journey App](https://www.patientjourneyapp.com) team, now part of [Legrand Care](https://www.legrand.com/care/int-en). I wrote the app's first line of code, and it has since guided more than a million patients through their treatment. I'm the tech lead, which mostly means working across the whole stack: architecture, code review, CI/CD, and keeping our Kubernetes clusters running.
