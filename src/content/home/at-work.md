I'm tech lead on the [Patient Journey App](https://www.patientjourneyapp.com) at [Interactive Studios](https://www.interactivestudios.nl). I wrote its first line of code. Today it's used by over 100 healthcare institutions worldwide and has guided more than a million patients through their treatment.

I work across the whole stack: architecture, code review, CI/CD, and keeping our Kubernetes clusters running. I also share what I know with the team and keep up with where the tech is heading.

I started coding at 14, writing little Windows tweak tools in Visual Basic. I went on to study computer science and joined Interactive Studios in 2013.
