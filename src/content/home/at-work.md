I started coding at 14, writing little Windows tweak tools in Visual Basic, and never really stopped. After studying computer science I joined [Interactive Studios](https://www.interactivestudios.nl) in 2013.

These days I work on the [Patient Journey App](https://www.patientjourneyapp.com). I wrote its first line of code, and it has since guided more than a million patients through their treatment. I'm the tech lead, which mostly means working across the whole stack: architecture, code review, CI/CD, and keeping our Kubernetes clusters running.
