a mail client in Rust that keeps your mailbox clean. Rules move mail into folders and delete sign-in codes and magic links once you're done with them, with a 30-day safety net; agents can propose rules, you approve them. Today it's an IMAP sync engine with a CLI. A keyboard-driven window is on its way, then an MCP server and sending mail.
