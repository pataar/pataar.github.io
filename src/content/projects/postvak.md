a mail client in Rust that keeps your mailbox clean. Rules move mail into folders and delete sign-in codes and magic links once you're done with them, with a 30-day safety net; agents can propose rules over MCP, you approve them. It runs headless as a background service or in Docker, with a keyboard-driven mail window for reading and a full CLI.
